How to Use

  • Select 1–5 images with the “⬆️ Choose Images” button.
  • Click one of the style buttons (Anime, Pixar, Comic, Disney).
  • Wait for the AI to render—your “before” thumbnails stay above.
  • Your “after” stylized images appear below with download links.
  • You get X credits per day; each stylization uses one credit.

RULES

  • Avoid groups with 6+ people unless its very detailed and up close where frame is filled (risk of fail render)
  • Make sure photos are crisp and not blurry
  • Vertical Photo: Make sure its vertical (edit tilt 90 degrees or enable auto tilt feature on your camera settings)
  • Ensure everyone is looking at camera, side profile/closed eyes increases change of bad render

Tips

  • Hold 'ctrl' to select multiple images at once or zoom in photo selctor
  • In rare cases program might generate an extra person-> Use different theme or crop photo
  • 6% error/failed render
  • 2D Animation/Anime sells best followed by 3D and Comic is very niche.
  • Learn how the AI works: what photos does it like? which style handles best lighting/poses? Find your style and Enjoy!

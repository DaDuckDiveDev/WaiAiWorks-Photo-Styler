How It Works

  • Pick 1–5 photos with the Choose Photos button.
  • Select a style — Anime Dream, Pixar Magic, Comic Hero, or Disney Glow.
  • Sit back while the AI transforms your photos. Your originals stay above for comparison.
  • Download your creations individually or all at once.
  • Each stylization uses one credit. Credits refresh daily.

For Best Results

  • Avoid large groups of 6+ people (higher chance of imperfect render).
  • Use sharp, well-lit photos — avoid blurry or dark shots.
  • Portrait orientation works best; rotate tilted photos before uploading.
  • Everyone facing the camera gives the cleanest result.

Tips & Tricks

  • Hold Ctrl to select multiple images at once in the file picker.
  • In rare cases the AI may add an extra person — try a different style or crop the photo.
  • Overall render error rate is around 6%.
  • Anime Dream tends to sell best; Comic Hero is great for dramatic shots.
  • Experiment! Each style handles lighting and poses differently — find your favourite.
